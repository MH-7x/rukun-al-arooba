## H2 — Safe and Responsible AC Disposal in Dubai

Old air conditioners contain refrigerant gases like R22 and R32. If they're removed incorrectly, those gases cause environmental damage. Our team handles **proper refrigerant recovery (degassing)** before dismantling and disposing of the unit. Metals and reusable components are recovered responsibly.

So when you sell your old AC to us, you're not just getting paid — you're also getting rid of it the right way, in line with UAE environmental regulations.
